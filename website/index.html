Logging

A keypad built for barbells.

No system keyboard, no fumbling. Weight and reps get their own number pad, with RIR built into the same tap and a rest timer running underneath.

  • Plate math for barbell and functional-trainer lifts
  • Edit a logged set's weight, reps, or RIR without adding a new one
  • New estimated 1RM flagged the moment you beat it
Programs

Rotations that run themselves.

Start from Push/Pull/Legs, Upper/Lower, a 4-day split, or a blank slate. VIGOR remembers where you left off and hands you the next day on Home.

  • Sets, exercises, and estimated duration per day
  • Muscle-volume breakdown before you commit to a program
  • Superset support for supersetted exercises
Recovery

Know what's actually fresh.

A daily stress score blends lift and cardio load into one 0–100 read, while per-muscle freshness tells you whether today is a squat day or a walk day.

  • 7-day stress trend, split by lift vs. cardio
  • Per-muscle recovery percentage, updated after every session
  • Personal records and e1RM history for every main lift
Daily coaching

Home tells you what to do today.

A quick daily check-in (sleep, mood, energy, caffeine) feeds Apple Health insights. Home then reads your fatigue and freshness to suggest the session that fits, or a deload when you need one.

  • Daily check-in with a streak and an optional reminder
  • “Train today” suggestion based on per-muscle freshness
  • Deload advice when fatigue stays high for days
  • All cardio (cycling, hiking, swimming) shows on the activity grid
Progress

Chase records, with a target.

Best estimated 1RMs on your main lifts, plus a projected next PR from your recent trend. Beat one and the app celebrates it, and velocity-based training tracks bar speed.

  • PR celebrations the moment a set beats your best
  • Projected next e1RM from your recent trend
  • Velocity-based training (VBT) bar tracking
  • Training-load evolution: compare program cycles to a baseline
Library

Every lift, tagged and searchable.

Barbell, dumbbell, machine, or bodyweight — the catalog is filterable by category, equipment, and muscle, so building a day never means guessing at names.

  • Filter by category, equipment, or target muscle
  • Add your own custom exercises alongside the catalog
  • Illustrated form reference on every entry
Connected

Built around Apple Health, not next to it.

Apple Health

Reads running, walking, hiking and cycling; writes finished strength sessions and body weight back, if you turn it on.

Apple Watch

A companion app logs sets live from your wrist, in step with the session running on your phone.

Widgets

Year-in-pixels, today's stress, and your next workout — on the Home Screen, StandBy and Lock Screen, each with its own style.

Make it yours
