Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. VIGOR has no accounts or cloud sync. Use the export or backup option in Settings to keep a copy.

How do I move to a new phone?

Create a backup from Settings, move the file to your new phone, and restore it from the same screen.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all local data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Apple Health isn't updating

Open Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → VIGOR and make sure the categories you want are enabled.

How do I import a program from the Program Builder?

Build your program in the Program Builder, export the JSON, and import it from the Programs tab in the app.
